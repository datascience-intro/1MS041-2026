Dataset: Bike Sharing, daily data (course teaching extract)

Local file: data/dc_bikeshare.csv
Original provider: UCI Machine Learning Repository.
Citation: Fanaee-T, H. (2013), Bike Sharing [Dataset], UCI Machine Learning
Repository, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5W894.
Related publication: Fanaee-T, Hadi, and Gama, Joao, "Event labeling combining
ensemble detectors and background knowledge", Progress in Artificial
Intelligence (2013), pp. 1-15, Springer Berlin Heidelberg,
doi:10.1007/s13748-013-0040-3. The dataset's readme asks publications that use
the data to cite this paper.
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
Credit requirement: cite the dataset and the publication when sharing the data,
an analysis, or an adapted copy.

Original subject: daily counts of rented bikes in the Capital Bikeshare system,
with weather and calendar information, for 2011 and 2012.

Rows and columns: 731 rows, one per day in date order, with no missing values.
- date: the day, from 2011-01-01 to 2012-12-31 (original field dteday).
- rentals: total bikes rented that day (original field cnt).
- temperature_c: temperature in degrees Celsius (original temp times 41,
  rounded to two decimals).
- humidity_pct: humidity in percent (original hum times 100, rounded to two
  decimals).
- wind_speed: wind speed (original windspeed times 67, rounded to two decimals).
  The readme gives the scaling but does not state a unit.
- weather: 1 clear or partly cloudy; 2 mist or cloud; 3 light rain or snow
  (original field weathersit). Its fourth category, heavy rain or snow, does
  not occur in the daily file.
- working_day: 1 if the day is neither a weekend nor a holiday, otherwise 0
  (original field workingday).

Transformation: produced from the original day.csv by the column selection,
renaming, and rescaling above. The original fields season, yr, mnth, holiday,
weekday, atemp, casual, and registered are omitted. The casual and registered
counts add up to the rentals count.

Fitness limitation: this is one system over two years, and consecutive days are
related. Ridership grew strongly over the period. The humidity value of
2011-03-10 is 0, which looks like a recording problem, and is kept as supplied.
Results do not automatically transfer to other cities or to later years, and
they do not show a causal effect of the weather.
