Dataset: Auto MPG (course teaching extract)

Local file: data/auto.csv
Original provider: UCI Machine Learning Repository.
Creator and citation: R. Quinlan (1993), Auto MPG, UCI Machine Learning
Repository, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5859H.
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
Credit requirement: cite Quinlan and the UCI dataset when sharing the data,
an analysis, or an adapted copy.

Original subject: city-cycle fuel consumption in miles per gallon for
automobiles, together with vehicle attributes. The local course extract has
398 rows and these seven columns: mpg, cylinders, displacement, horsepower,
weight, acceleration, and model-year. It omits the UCI origin and car-name
fields. The local file has two missing horsepower values.

Transformation limitation: the repository does not preserve a complete
record of how this seven-column teaching extract was produced from the UCI
files. Treat the UCI dataset as the source and the local file as an adapted
course copy, but record the local schema and missing-value count rather than
claiming that it is byte-identical to a current UCI download.

Fitness limitation: the observations concern historical model years 1970
through 1982. Vehicle models, technology, regulation, and collection practice
change over time, and related vehicles may not be independent. Results from
this extract do not automatically transfer to current vehicles, every market,
or a causal effect of a vehicle attribute.
