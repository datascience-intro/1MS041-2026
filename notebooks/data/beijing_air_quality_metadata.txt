Extraction rule: retain the source header and every source row whose No value
is from 193 through 264, inclusive. Preserve all 18 columns and source order.
This gives 72 consecutive hourly records from Aotizhongxin, beginning at
2013-03-09 00:00 and ending at 2013-03-11 23:00. No row was cleaned, imputed,
reordered, or otherwise changed for the local extract.

Schema and units: No is the source row number. The fields year, month, day,
and hour identify the recorded hour. PM2.5, PM10, SO2, NO2, CO, and O3 are
pollutant concentrations in micrograms per cubic metre. TEMP and DEWP are in
degrees Celsius, PRES is in hPa, RAIN is in mm, wd is wind direction, WSPM is
wind speed in metres per second, and station is the monitoring-site name.

Missing-value convention: the literal text NA means that a measurement is
missing. Missingness is not a zero measurement and is not by itself an invalid
row. This local excerpt contains exactly one missing value, in PM10 at source
row No 205.

Fitness limitation: this is a three-day consecutive excerpt from one station,
not a random sample of Beijing, China, or later years. Adjacent hourly records
may be dependent. Weather, emissions, instruments, and monitoring practice may
change over time. The excerpt cannot by itself support a citywide, causal, or
current-air-quality claim.
